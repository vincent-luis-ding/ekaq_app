Dual Delivery Technologies

Two engineered formats, one principle: keep high-activity molecules stable until the moment of consumption, then deliver them with maximal bio-availability.

01

Push-Cap Fresh-Dispense System

High-activity sensitive molecules — such as monohydrate creatine — are held dry and isolated inside a sealed cap reservoir, fully separated from water. Press the cap, release the actives, shake and drink.

  • ◆Dry–liquid separation locks potency
  • ◆No hydrolysis, no sedimentation
  • ◆Fresh-mixed on demand
02

Precision Stick Packs

Microencapsulated fine powder, precision-balanced per daily serving — 3000 mg creatine, taurine, chelated minerals and microencapsulated D3. Add to 250–300 mL ambient water and stir.

  • ◆Instant dissolve, zero sediment
